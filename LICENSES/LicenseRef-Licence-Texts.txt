The texts of licences, and the licence and copyright notices of other
projects, copied unchanged: scripts/licence-texts/ (the standard texts of
the licences, from the SPDX licence list) and app/public/NOTICES.txt
(Erevos's licence and AUTHORS.md, and each package's own licence files).

Each licence text belongs to whoever wrote it (the Free Software
Foundation, the Apache Software Foundation, the Mozilla Foundation and
others), and each notice to the makers it names. They are here so that
every copy of Erevos carries them, as the licences ask, and may be copied
and given on unchanged, as each of them allows.
