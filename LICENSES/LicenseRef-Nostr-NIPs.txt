Example events from the Nostr protocol's NIP-17 text
(https://github.com/nostr-protocol/nips, file 17.md), copied unchanged into
crates/erevos-nostr/tests/ so the tests can check Erevos against them.

That repository states no licence of its own. The examples are part of the
protocol's public description, used here only by the tests and not shipped in
the app. Their authors are the contributors to the Nostr NIPs.
